How channels work

Each agent that connects to {{.MCPURL}} calls open_channel and then pulls work with claim_request. Requests are not pushed: the next free channel that serves the requested model claims the oldest, highest-priority request. A channel with no activity for the configured stale period goes offline and its in-flight requests are returned to the queue. Use drain to stop a channel from taking new work while it finishes what it holds.
